# Final GREEN Definition

Linqa is GA eligible only when G0-G5, Expo 57 migration, production dependency security, signed iOS and Android physical-device certification, exact artifact/revision binding, evidence freshness, release-test completeness, external reviews, operational assurance, canary/rollback proof, and formal approvals are all GREEN.

No environment boolean may manufacture GREEN. The promoted artifact must be the exact signed artifact that was tested and reviewed; rebuilding after evidence collection creates a new release candidate and invalidates affected evidence.

The Copilot-authored native Android client (`clients/android`, package `com.linqa.app`) is the sole official Galaxy launch medium. The separate Expo client remains a required cross-platform release surface but is not substituted as the official Android launcher.
