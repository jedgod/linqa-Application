# DSAR Runbook

1. Receive request.
2. Verify identity.
3. Resolve jurisdiction and due date.
4. Check active legal holds.
5. Process system inventory steps.
6. Generate access/export artifact where required.
7. Apply deletion/correction/opt-out action.
8. Verify downstream deletion where applicable.
9. Record evidence.
10. Complete request.

## Deletion exceptions
Do not silently delete information that is subject to:
- legal hold;
- statutory retention;
- fraud/security retention;
- billing/tax retention.

Any exception must be documented and surfaced as a bounded legal exception,
not used as a blanket denial.
