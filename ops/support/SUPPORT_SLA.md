# Support SLA

URGENT
- initial response: 15 minutes
- escalation: security/operations as applicable

HIGH
- initial response: 1 hour

NORMAL
- initial response: 8 business hours

LOW
- initial response: 2 business days

## Support access
Support personnel can access:
- account identifiers;
- device/session status;
- delivery metadata;
- capability states;
- consented diagnostic bundles.

Support personnel cannot access:
- private keys;
- plaintext messages;
- decrypted Vault content;
- full card credentials;
- precise location unless an authorized emergency workflow explicitly permits it.
