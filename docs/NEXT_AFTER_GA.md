# After GA

Once General Availability is complete, move to a continuous production program:

- quarterly cryptographic review;
- annual or major-release penetration tests;
- continuous dependency and container scanning;
- quarterly disaster-recovery drills;
- SLO/error-budget review;
- privacy request SLA monitoring;
- SOC 2 audit evidence maintenance;
- regional capacity planning;
- enterprise support maturity;
- abuse/fraud model tuning;
- post-launch retention and deletion verification;
- monthly app-store release cadence;
- controlled feature experimentation.
