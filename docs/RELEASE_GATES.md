# Release Gates

## Linqa 1.0
- Real phone/email authentication and passkeys
- Verified device enrollment
- Encrypted messaging round trip
- Encrypted groups, attachments, and voice notes
- Audio/video calling through TURN
- Multi-device sync
- Backup and restore
- Privacy and trust/safety tests
- Independent security review

## Linqa 2.0
- Screen sharing across supported clients
- Whiteboard convergence and replay safety
- Tasks, polls, events, and communities
- Teleprompter remains local-only
- Recorded video always labeled non-live
- AI privacy modes enforced
- Accessibility and moderation review

## Linqa 3.0
- Organization isolation
- SSO and SCIM
- Enterprise policy enforcement
- Device compliance
- Retention and legal-hold review
- Verified identity revocation
- Secure bridge threat model
- Enterprise billing and audit controls
