# Linqa Three Production Milestones

## Linqa 1.0 — Secure Communications
Authentication, device enrollment, encrypted messaging, groups, attachments,
voice notes, disappearing messages, encrypted audio/video calls, multi-device
sync, encrypted backup, privacy, safety, and subscription foundations.

## Linqa 2.0 — Collaboration and AI
Screen sharing, whiteboard, tasks, polls, call scheduling, live captions,
translation, AI summaries, teleprompter, recorded replies, communities,
events, business profiles, and advanced moderation.

## Linqa 3.0 — Enterprise, Identity, and Compliance
Organizations, workspaces, SSO, SCIM, policy enforcement, device compliance,
audit controls, retention, legal hold, verified identity credentials,
secure organization bridges, enterprise analytics, and billing.

Baseline encryption and account security must remain available to all users.

See `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md` for what must be true before each milestone ships,
and `docs/EXECUTION_AND_LAUNCH.md` for how features are promoted/rolled back
once a milestone is in progress.
