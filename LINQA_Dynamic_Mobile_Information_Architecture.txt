LINQA — Dynamic Mobile Information Architecture
================================================

Purpose
-------
A scalable, dynamic mobile information architecture for the Linqa platform. The design preserves a simple mobile navigation model while allowing Linqa to expand across messaging, calls, discovery, CountryLinqa, LinqaSend, LinqaVault, Wallet, AI, Calendar, business services, safety, and future modules.

================================================

5. DYNAMIC NAVIGATION RULES
================================================

1. Keep the bottom bar limited to five primary controls.
2. Do not add a permanent bottom tab for every Linqa module.
3. Surface frequently used services dynamically on Home.
4. Allow users to pin favorite services.
5. Preserve one-tap access to Calls and Chats.
6. Use the center "+" for creation and context-sensitive actions.
7. Use the Linqa tab as the complete service launcher.
8. Use universal search for cross-module navigation.
9. Support deep links into every major module.
10. Preserve navigation state when switching modules.
11. Show badges only for actionable/unread information.
12. Allow server-driven feature availability, but never silently override security/privacy controls.
13. Hide unavailable or unauthorized enterprise/admin features.
14. Respect account-specific entitlements and permissions.
15. Keep sensitive Vault and payment actions behind their required authorization gates.

================================================
6. PERSONALIZATION
================================================

The Linqa Hub may learn interface preferences without changing security guarantees.

Examples:
- Frequent LinqaSend user → surface Send Money on Home.
- Frequent caller → prioritize Recent Calls and scheduled calls.
- Heavy Vault user → show storage and intake cards.
- CountryLinqa user → prioritize regional discovery.
- Business account → surface Team Workspace and Business Vault.
- Upcoming meeting → surface Calendar and Join Call.
- Pending transfer → show transfer status.
- Unread priority conversation → surface Continue Conversation.

User controls:
- Pin service
- Unpin service
- Reorder quick actions
- Hide recommendation
- Reset Home layout
- Disable personalization where supported

================================================
11. DESIGN PRINCIPLE
================================================

The architecture should feel simple even as Linqa becomes larger.

The user should not need to understand the platform's internal module architecture. Linqa should surface the correct action at the correct moment while keeping every service discoverable through the Linqa Hub and universal command/search layer.

The model is designed to scale without continuously adding permanent navigation tabs.
